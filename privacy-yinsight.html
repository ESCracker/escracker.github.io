易卜智答 YInsight 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 17 日 · 开发者：EShane（escracker@outlook.com）

本政策说明“易卜智答”（包名 com.escracker.yinsight，以下简称“本应用”）如何处理您的信息。本应用不要求注册账号，开发者不运营任何收集用户数据的服务器。

1. 仅保存在您设备上的信息

  • 占卜记录、AI 解读结果、追问对话、语言等设置；
  • 您填写的生辰资料（出生日期、时辰、性别、出生地）；
  • 自定义 API 的地址和模型名称；API 密钥使用 Android Keystore 加密保存；
  • 每日免费 AI 次数的计数。

这些信息不会上传给开发者。您可以在“记录”页删除记录，或卸载应用清除全部数据。

2. 使用 AI 解读时发送的信息

免费 AI（默认）

当您使用免费 AI 时，本应用将以下内容通过加密连接（HTTPS）发送给阿里云百炼平台的通义千问模型以生成解读：您输入的问题、问题分类、程序计算出的盘面（卦象、奇门盘、塔罗牌等），以及在您开启“八字”时的生辰资料。请求不包含您的账号、设备标识或位置信息。这些数据由阿里云依照其条款处理，详见 阿里云隐私政策。开发者不会保存或查看您的问题内容。请勿在问题中填写身份证号、联系方式等敏感个人信息。

自定义 API

如果您在设置中改用自定义 API，上述内容会直接发送到您自己配置的服务商，不经过开发者。其数据处理方式由该服务商负责。

本地模型

使用本地模型时，解读完全在您的设备上完成，不会发送问题内容。下载模型文件时，会从模型托管网站获取文件。

3. 广告

本应用使用 Google AdMob 展示广告（“记录”页横幅，以及您主动点击的激励视频）。AdMob 可能收集 IP 地址（用于推断大致位置）、Android 广告 ID 等设备标识、广告互动及诊断信息，用于投放广告、统计和防欺诈。在需要同意的地区（如欧洲经济区、英国），本应用会通过 Google 的用户消息平台（UMP）征求您的同意，您可在“设置 → 广告隐私设置”中随时修改。您也可以在系统设置中重置或删除广告 ID。详见 Google 广告政策。

4. 购买

“永久去广告”通过 Google Play 结算完成。付款信息由 Google 处理，开发者无法获得。本应用仅向 Google Play 查询您是否已购买，以便提供相应权益。

5. 儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

6. 内容声明

传统术数与 AI 解读仅供文化研究、娱乐与个人反思，不构成医疗、法律、投资或人身安全方面的专业建议。

7. 政策更新与联系方式

本政策更新时会在此页面公布。如有疑问，请联系 escracker@outlook.com。

YInsight Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026 · Developer: EShane (escracker@outlook.com)

This policy explains how YInsight (package com.escracker.yinsight, “the app”) handles your information. The app requires no account, and the developer runs no server that collects user data.

1. Information kept only on your device

  • Reading history, AI interpretations, follow-up conversations and settings;
  • Birth details you enter (date, hour, gender, birthplace);
  • Custom API endpoint and model name; the API key is encrypted with Android Keystore;
  • The daily free-AI usage counter.

This information is not sent to the developer. You can delete records on the Records tab or uninstall the app to remove everything.

2. Information sent for AI readings

Free AI (default)

When you use the free AI, the app sends your question, its category, the charts calculated by the app, and—if you enable Ba-Zi—your birth details over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to Alibaba Cloud's Qwen models (Model Studio / Bailian) to generate the reading. The request does not include your account, device identifiers, or location. Alibaba Cloud processes this data under its own terms; see the Alibaba Cloud Privacy Policy. The developer does not store or view your questions. Please do not include sensitive personal information such as ID numbers or contact details in your questions.

Custom API

If you switch to a custom API, the same content is sent directly to the provider you configured, not to the developer. That provider is responsible for how it handles the data.

On-device model

With an on-device model, readings are generated entirely on your device and your questions are not sent anywhere. Downloading a model fetches files from the model hosting site.

3. Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob (a banner on the Records tab and rewarded videos you choose to watch). AdMob may collect your IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Where consent is required (for example, the EEA and UK), the app asks for it through Google's User Messaging Platform; you can change your choice anytime under Settings → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in system settings. See How Google uses data in advertising.

4. Purchases

“Remove Ads” is purchased through Google Play billing. Payment details are handled by Google and are never available to the developer. The app only checks with Google Play whether you own a purchase so it can provide the benefit.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

6. Content notice

Traditional divination and AI readings are for cultural study, entertainment and personal reflection only, and are not medical, legal, financial or safety advice.

7. Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions: escracker@outlook.com.
