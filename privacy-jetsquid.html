鱿往直前 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 16 日 · 开发者：EShane（escracker@outlook.com）

本政策说明游戏“鱿往直前”（包名 com.shane.jetsquid，以下简称“本游戏”）如何处理您的信息。本游戏不要求注册账号，开发者不运营任何收集玩家数据的服务器。

1. 仅保存在您设备上的信息

游戏进度、珍珠数量、已解锁内容、成就、竞速成绩、水族馆和设置等，只保存在您的设备上，不会上传给开发者。卸载游戏或清除应用数据会删除这些信息。

2. 好友挑战码

“幽灵竞速”和“珊瑚冲刺”的挑战码由游戏在本机生成，内容是您这一局的游戏轨迹和成绩，不包含个人信息。只有当您主动点击“复制”并自行发送给好友时，它才会离开您的设备。复制时游戏会使用系统剪贴板。

3. 广告

本游戏使用 Google AdMob 展示广告，包括一局结束后的插屏广告，以及您主动选择观看的激励视频。AdMob 可能收集 IP 地址（用于推断大致位置）、Android 广告 ID 等设备标识、广告互动及诊断信息，用于投放广告、统计和防欺诈。在需要同意的地区（如欧洲经济区、英国），游戏会通过 Google 的用户消息平台（UMP）征求您的同意，您可在“设置 → 广告隐私设置”中随时修改。您也可以在系统设置中重置或删除广告 ID。详见 Google 广告政策。

4. 购买

珍珠礼包、解锁世界/角色、去除广告均通过 Google Play 结算完成。付款信息由 Google 处理，开发者无法获得。游戏仅向 Google Play 查询购买状态，以便发放和恢复相应内容。

5. 字体

游戏界面会从 Google Fonts 加载字体，此时您的设备会向 Google 服务器发送请求（包括 IP 地址）。详见 Google Fonts 隐私说明。

6. 儿童

本游戏不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。如您是监护人并认为孩子提供了个人信息，请联系我们。

7. 政策更新与联系方式

本政策更新时会在此页面公布。如有疑问，请联系 escracker@outlook.com。

Jet Squid Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026 · Developer: EShane (escracker@outlook.com)

This policy explains how the game Jet Squid (package com.shane.jetsquid, “the game”) handles your information. The game requires no account, and the developer runs no server that collects player data.

1. Information kept only on your device

Game progress, pearls, unlocks, achievements, race times, your aquarium and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

2. Friend challenge codes

Challenge codes for Ghost Race and Coral Dash are generated on your device and contain only your run's path and score—no personal information. They leave your device only if you tap “Copy” and send them to a friend yourself. Copying uses the system clipboard.

3. Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, including interstitial ads after a run and rewarded videos you choose to watch. AdMob may collect your IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Where consent is required (for example, the EEA and UK), the game asks for it through Google's User Messaging Platform; you can change your choice anytime under Settings → Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in system settings. See How Google uses data in advertising.

4. Purchases

Pearl packs, world and character unlocks, and ad removal are purchased through Google Play billing. Payment details are handled by Google and are never available to the developer. The game only checks your purchase status with Google Play to deliver and restore content.

5. Fonts

The game loads fonts from Google Fonts, which sends a request (including your IP address) to Google's servers. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

6. Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us.

7. Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions: escracker@outlook.com.
