狼影重重 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 24 日 · 开发者：EShane（escracker@outlook.com）

本政策说明游戏“狼影重重”（包名 com.shane.wolfshadows，以下简称“本游戏”）如何处理您的信息。本游戏是您与 8 位 AI 玩家进行的狼人杀文字游戏。本游戏不要求注册账号，开发者不运营任何收集玩家数据的服务器。

1. 仅保存在您设备上的信息

  • 设置：发言节奏、语言、AI 来源（游戏自带 AI 或您自己的接口）等；
  • 每日免广告局数、开局记录等计数；
  • 您自己的 AI 接口地址和模型名称；API 密钥使用 Android Keystore 加密保存，游戏界面无法读取明文；
  • “去广告”购买状态的本地记录。

对局内容（发言、投票、AI 的推理过程）只在这一局进行时存在于内存中，不会保存。以上信息不会上传给开发者。卸载游戏或清除应用数据会删除这些信息。

2. AI 玩家发言时发送的信息

游戏自带 AI（默认）

为了让 AI 玩家思考和发言，本游戏会通过加密连接（HTTPS）把当前对局的内容发送给阿里云百炼平台（Model Studio）上的大语言模型（例如 DeepSeek V4 Pro）。发送的内容包括：本局规则、座位与身份信息、各玩家的发言（包括您在游戏中输入的发言和留言）、投票和夜间行动结果。请求不包含您的账号、设备标识或位置信息。这些数据由阿里云依照其条款处理，详见 阿里云隐私政策。开发者不会保存或查看您的发言。请勿在发言中填写真实姓名、联系方式、身份证号等个人信息。

您自己的 AI 接口

如果您在游戏中设置并使用自己的 AI 接口，上述对局内容会直接发送到您填写的地址，不经过开发者。测试连接和获取模型列表时，游戏也会用您的密钥访问该地址。这些数据的处理方式由该服务商负责。

3. 广告

本游戏使用 Google AdMob 展示广告：用游戏自带 AI 开局前的激励视频，以及对局中屏幕底部的横幅广告。AdMob 可能收集 IP 地址（用于推断大致位置）、Android 广告 ID 等设备标识、广告互动及诊断信息，用于投放广告、统计和防欺诈。在需要同意的地区（如欧洲经济区、英国、瑞士），游戏会通过 Google 的用户消息平台（UMP）征求您的同意，您可以在首页的“隐私与广告设置”中随时修改。您也可以在系统设置中重置或删除广告 ID。详见 Google 广告政策。

4. 购买

“去广告”通过 Google Play 结算完成。付款信息由 Google 处理，开发者无法获得。游戏仅向 Google Play 查询您是否已购买，以便提供和恢复相应权益。

5. 网络时间

为了正确计算“每天”的免广告局数，游戏会读取 AI 服务响应中的标准时间，不会因此发送额外的个人信息。

6. 儿童

本游戏面向一般受众，并非专门面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。如果您认为儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请通过下方邮箱联系，我们会尽快处理。

7. AI 内容声明

游戏中的角色均为虚构人物，发言由 AI 自动生成，可能不准确、不合逻辑或出乎意料，仅供娱乐，不代表开发者观点。

8. 您的权利

您可以随时卸载游戏删除本地数据、清除自己的 AI 接口设置、修改广告同意选择，或联系我们询问数据处理情况。根据所在地区的法律（如 GDPR、CCPA），您可能享有访问、更正、删除数据以及拒绝个性化广告的权利。

9. 政策更新与联系方式

本政策更新时会在此页面公布并修改生效日期。如有疑问，请联系 escracker@outlook.com。

Wolf Shadows Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Developer: EShane (escracker@outlook.com)

This policy explains how Wolf Shadows (package com.shane.wolfshadows, “the game”) handles your information. The game is a text-based werewolf game you play with 8 AI players. It requires no account, and the developer runs no server that collects player data.

1. Information kept only on your device

  • Settings: speaking pace, language, AI source (built-in AI or your own API), etc.;
  • Counters such as daily ad-free games and game starts;
  • Your own AI API endpoint and model name; the API key is encrypted with Android Keystore and cannot be read by the game screen;
  • A local record of whether you bought “Remove ads”.

Game content (speeches, votes, the AIs’ reasoning) exists in memory only while a game is running and is not saved. None of this is sent to the developer. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes it.

2. Information sent when AI players speak

Built-in AI (default)

So the AI players can think and speak, the game sends the current game’s content over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to large language models (for example DeepSeek V4 Pro) on Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Bailian). This includes the rules, seats and roles, every player’s speeches (including what you type in the game), votes and night results. Requests do not include your account, device identifiers or location. Alibaba Cloud processes this data under its own terms; see the Alibaba Cloud Privacy Policy. The developer does not store or view what you type. Please don’t include real names, contact details, ID numbers or other personal information in your speeches.

Your own AI API

If you set up and use your own AI API, the same game content is sent directly to the address you entered, not to the developer. Testing the connection and loading the model list also contact that address with your key. That provider is responsible for how it handles the data.

3. Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: a rewarded video before games that use the built-in AI, and a banner at the bottom of the screen during games. AdMob may collect your IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Where consent is required (for example, the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the game asks for it through Google’s User Messaging Platform; you can change your choice anytime with “Privacy & ad settings” on the home screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in system settings. See How Google uses data in advertising.

4. Purchases

“Remove ads” is purchased through Google Play billing. Payment details are handled by Google and are never available to the developer. The game only checks with Google Play whether you own the purchase so it can provide and restore the benefit.

5. Network time

To count “daily” ad-free games correctly, the game reads the standard time from AI service responses. No extra personal information is sent for this.

6. Children

The game is for a general audience, is not directed to children under 13, and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will handle it promptly.

7. AI content notice

All characters are fictional. Their speech is generated automatically by AI and may be inaccurate, illogical or unexpected. It is for entertainment only and does not represent the developer’s views.

8. Your rights

You can uninstall the game to delete local data, remove your own AI API settings, change your ad consent, or contact us about how your data is handled. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete data and to opt out of personalized ads.

9. Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: escracker@outlook.com.
